## Visual accuracy rules

- Label recreated systems and routes as illustrative.
- Do not show real credentials, account numbers, operational SWIFT screens, or actionable attack steps.
- Do not imply SWIFT itself was hacked or the New York Fed’s systems were breached.
- The printer is a visibility and incident-response detail, not the transfer mechanism.
- Keep attribution tied to U.S. legal allegations; avoid a visual “verdict.”
- Use archival material only when date, location, and rights are verified. No invented surveillance footage or employee behavior.
